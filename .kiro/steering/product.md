# SoukAI — Product steering

## Vision
Every micro-merchant in North Africa should be able to list a product
for sale in under a minute, in their own language, from their phone —
no copywriting skill, no pricing guesswork, no desktop needed.

## Audience
Instagram / WhatsApp sellers in Algeria and the Maghreb: individuals
selling clothes, electronics, cosmetics and home goods through DMs.
They are not developers. They are not on a laptop. Connectivity is
spotty. Arabic is their first language; French is the second language
of commerce.

## Tone
- **Arabic-first, always.** Arabic copy is the primary text; French is
  secondary, never the other way around.
- Warm, direct, market-stall voice: short sentences, clear prices,
  honest condition descriptions. No hype, no fake urgency.
- Prices in DZD by default. Never invent a price the merchant did not
  anchor — suggestions come from comparable ranges the merchant saw,
  scaled by condition.

## Product principles
1. One photo in, one listing out. Every extra tap is a lost merchant.
2. Offline-first: the core flow must work with no network.
3. The merchant is the editor: generated copy is a draft, never final.
4. Free tiers only: the merchant pays nothing, the app costs nothing
   to run at hackathon scale.
